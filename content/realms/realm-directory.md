# Realms of Eldaven

Browse the established realms and major political powers currently represented in Arkenfell. Use the directory search to find a realm by name, culture, government, or recurring theme.

## Major Continental Powers

### [Kingdom of Valenreach](#kingdom-of-valenreach)
A wealthy royal power whose Crown, noble houses, military orders, mage institutions, roads, and rural holdings bind together much of central Eldaven.

### [The Dreadmark Dominion](#dreadmark-dominion)
An eastern military dominion built on hierarchy, forced labor, slavery, and vast legions.

### [The Tide Empire](#tide-empire)
An ancient northern empire of fleets, river cities, dragonriders, and long imperial continuity.

### [The Lythael Elven Dominion](#lythael-elven-dominion)
An ancient southern elven realm shaped by old forests, noble matriarchs, and long political memory.

### [The Solmire Concord](#solmire-concord)
A western merchant republic whose influence flows through trade, contracts, convoys, and credit.

### [The Solarian Theocracy](#solarian-theocracy)
A southeastern religious state ruled by a High Synod and influential through temples, healing, and doctrine.

### [The Arcanum Ascendancy](#arcanum-ascendancy)
An independent mage-ruled state centered on academies, magical classification, research, and arbitration.

## Other Established Realms

### [Emberhorn Accord](#emberhorn-accord)
An established Eldaven polity associated with the Cinderhorn ruling family, contract traditions, and fire-linked power.

### [Ashen Maw](#ashen-maw)
An established Eldaven polity whose fuller public profile will grow as more player-safe canon is documented.

### [Coral Throne](#coral-throne)
A pearl-and-tide court represented by the Pearl-Crown ruling family and its maritime traditions.

### [Stormpearl Isles](#stormpearl-isles)
An island realm shaped by ships, reefs, storms, lighthouse families, privateers, and free captains.

### [Fangbound Clans](#fangbound-clans)
A clan-based power represented through the Great Fang Moot and prominent Stormhoof leadership.

### [Nightglass Principality](#nightglass-principality)
A principality shaped by an old ruling line, inherited obligations, curfews, escorts, and blood-contract traditions.

### [Cloudspire Dominion](#cloudspire-dominion)
A highland or cliff-linked dominion whose politics include route law, landing rights, storm charters, and noble houses.

### [Ironroot Holds](#ironroot-holds)
A dwarven federation of holds associated with clan benches, oathcraft, deep roads, mines, and rune-working.

### [Eldmark Kingdom](#eldmark-kingdom)
An established kingdom known through its Lawstone dynasty, remembered law, beacon wardens, and river household warriors.

### [Hollowmere Confederacy](#hollowmere-confederacy)
A confederacy whose agreements, councils, bridges, ferry roads, and ford militias shape its public political life.

### [Palegrave Crown](#palegrave-crown)
A northern crown associated with cairn law, inherited claims, old genealogies, cold lands, and martial leadership.
