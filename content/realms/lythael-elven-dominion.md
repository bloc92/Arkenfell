# The Lythael Elven Dominion

The Lythael Elven Dominion lies far to the south beyond the central human kingdoms, across vast ancient forests, hidden cities, and carefully guarded woodland roads.

It is one of the oldest surviving realms in the known world. The Lythael royal line has ruled for more than a thousand years within a society traditionally guided by powerful noble matriarchs and long-lived houses whose political memory reaches far beyond a human lifetime.

Lythael maintains formal relations with neighboring powers but favors patience, stability, etiquette, and long-term strategy over open displays of force. Its courts often regard haste as poor judgment, and many within the Dominion quietly consider their civilization older and more refined than the younger human realms.
