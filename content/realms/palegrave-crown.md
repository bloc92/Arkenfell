# The Palegrave Crown

The Palegrave Crown is a frost-giant polity whose public authority is tied to crown kinship, cairn law, burial claims, genealogies, old treaties, and remembered rights carried through spoken and stone-kept tradition rather than ordinary royal bureaucracy.

## Crown and Cairn Law

Palegrave Crownhold is a center of rule. Cairn-law speakers preserve genealogies, treaty names, burial rights, and claims that determine who can speak for old obligations.

**Yska Icecrown** is the Frost-heir and war-daughter of the Palegrave Crown. **Orun Cairn-Tongue** serves as a cairn-law speaker and keeper of old genealogies and treaty memory.

Cold Arcana and Earth Arcana are represented among its high-ranking figures, but Palegrave identity is defined as much by memory, kinship, burial law, and inherited obligation as by physical power.

## What awaits in play

Use it for frost ruins, giant legacies, ancient oaths, northern expeditions, lost halls, winter diplomacy, and powers that may wake slowly but move with enormous weight when roused.

These are possibilities rather than a required story path; a campaign may encounter the country through travel, diplomacy, conflict, trade, personal ties, or news from elsewhere in Eldaven.
