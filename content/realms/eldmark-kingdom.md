# Eldmark Kingdom

Eldmark Kingdom is a western mainland and island-linked realm shaped by cold rivers, pine ridges, timber halls, stone law-courts, beacon towers, fortified river towns, hill forts, ancestral barrows, saga-marked boundary stones, raiding history, and hard coastal weather.

## Law and Defense

Lawspeakers, beacon wardens, barrow-keepers, household warriors, road guards, oath witnesses, farmers, fishers, and raiders all belong to Eldmark's public life. Authority is tied closely to remembered law, household obligation, visible defense, beacon networks, royal law-stones, and the ability to keep crossings and coastal routes secure.

**Crown Prince Eydren Lawstone** is heir to the kingdom and serves as a beacon-war captain, trained among beacon wardens and river household warriors.

## Religion

Eldmark has no single named divine benefactor currently established as canon. Public devotion centers on storm shrines, ancestor oaths, royal law-stones, and war-dead barrows.
