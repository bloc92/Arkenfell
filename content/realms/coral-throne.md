# The Coral Throne

The Coral Throne is a reef-bound maritime power whose influence moves through coasts, islands, lagoons, sea courts, and protected routes rather than ordinary inland borders. Its society includes reef cities, tide temples, pearl nobility, sea-elven communities, merfolk envoys, lagoon markets, leviathan shrines, storm-protected harbors, and coral halls.

## Sea Law and Trade

The Throne's power depends on navigation, reef law, tide obligations, marine diplomacy, pearl wealth, and old sea pacts. It trades in pearls, coralcraft, medicines, rare shells, deepwater metals, tide blessings, navigation knowledge, and safe passage.

The realm is not wholly underwater. Surface harbors, reefs, lagoons, island courts, and submerged holdings form one political world connected by water.

## Religion

Its benefactor goddess is **Neryssan, the Coral Crown**, patron of reef law, sea courts, tide obligations, ocean spirits, submerged sovereignty, pearl tribute, and old marine pacts.
