# The Fangbound Clans

The Fangbound Clans are a broad eastern clan culture spread especially across the Thundergrass Steppe and Wolfroad Marches. Their peoples include orcish holds, beastkin families, ogre pact-bands, fortified well-keepers, monster herders, mounted clans, shrine keepers, hunters, and champion-led war camps.

They are not a single palace-centered state and they do not have one ruler whose approval represents every clan.

## Clan Honor

Fangbound life is shaped by kinship, challenge, herd rights, guest law, reputation, migration, hunting routes, shared wells, and the expectation that authority must remain worthy of obedience. Some clans raid, some trade, some protect caravans, and some remain settled around wells or strongholds.

Chieftains, shamans, matriarchs, beastlords, champions, and elders may all hold authority depending on the clan. Foreign titles carry little weight by themselves; courage, generosity, useful strength, kept obligations, and visible proof matter more.

## Religion

The Fangbound Clans honor **Khorvash, the Thunderhoof**, associated with mounted clans, herd rights, raids, monster hunts, strength, challenge, migration, kinship, and proof of worth.
