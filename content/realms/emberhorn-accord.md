# The Emberhorn Accord

The Emberhorn Accord is a civilized demon-blooded nation of volcanic terraces, obsidian cities, contract courts, red-gold temples, ash gardens, fortified roads, infernal academies, noble houses, merchant families, citizen militias, and public festivals.

## Society

Horns, tails, fire rites, infernal ancestry, passionate public argument, contract magic, and old demon lineages are ordinary parts of life in the Accord rather than evidence of evil. The nation places high value on binding word, personal strength, controlled passion, debt, hospitality, house honor, citizen protection, and judgment by conduct rather than foreign superstition.

A demon judge may be fair, a contract may protect the weak, and a heated debate may still follow rules stricter than those of a quieter human court. The Accord can be dangerous, proud, and unforgiving without being a monster kingdom.

## Religion

The Accord's benefactor goddess is **Veyrasha, the Unbound Flame**, associated with pacts, ambition, self-mastery, chosen identity, renaming, emancipation, and freedom from inherited damnation.
