# The Cloudspire Dominion

The Cloudspire Dominion is a highland and cliff society built around vertical travel, wind bridges, storm routes, landing rights, sky roads, beacons, cliff houses, and the practical dangers of living where a failed rope or bridge can kill.

## Authority and Routes

Cloudspire authority is exercised through councils, route law, remembered precedent, charters, landing rights, bridge obligations, and the people capable of keeping high roads usable. Cloudspire Aerie serves as a major political and administrative center, with institutions such as the Aerie Council, Flight Records, Wing Muster, and Seven Winds Shrine.

Storm-route charters, bridge rights, lift access, flight records, and alliances between cliff houses can carry both economic and political weight.

## Religion

The Dominion honors **Auraleth, the Sky-Bound Oath**, associated with cliffs, wind bridges, storm beacons, griffin riders, falling risks, lift-rights, sky roads, balance, engineering courage, and promises made where the ground cannot protect you.

## What awaits in play

Use it for sky travel, airship politics, floating ruins, storm monsters, aerial duels, weather rites, and nobles whose grace hides ruthless practicality.

These are possibilities rather than a required story path; a campaign may encounter the country through travel, diplomacy, conflict, trade, personal ties, or news from elsewhere in Eldaven.
