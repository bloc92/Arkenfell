# The Ironroot Holds

The Ironroot Holds are a dwarven federation shaped by clan authority, oathcraft, deep roads, mines, tunnel rights, durable work, rune traditions, forge law, and the expectation that obligations should survive the people who make them.

## Rule and Craft

The federation is led by **High Thane Brunna Anvilroot** from Kar Anvilroot. Clan benches, forge-rights, ancestor precedent, road debts, mine claims, survey records, shield companies, and practical engineering all matter to Ironroot politics.

Ironroot culture respects competence, honest materials, repaired mistakes, and promises that can withstand pressure. A map may be a legal argument, a tunnel a political asset, and a forge name as important as a court title.

## Religion

The Holds strongly revere **Bravik, the Hammer Saint**, god of forge, craft, stonework, mines, fortresses, honest labor, builders, smiths, endurance, shieldwalls, and properly made things.

## What awaits in play

Use the Holds for craft politics, underground expeditions, clan debts, forge secrets, sealed lower roads, monster breaches, and stubborn allies who demand proof before trust.

These are possibilities rather than a required story path; a campaign may encounter the country through travel, diplomacy, conflict, trade, personal ties, or news from elsewhere in Eldaven.
