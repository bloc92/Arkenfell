# The Ashen Maw

The Ashen Maw is an independent demon nation bordering the Emberhorn Accord. Its people organize through clans, strongholds, warbands, blood ties, oath circles, local rulers, and temporary coalitions rather than a single heavily bureaucratic state.

## Authority and Law

Maw authority rests on kin loyalty, strength, courage, reputation, guest law, blood obligation, martial achievement, and a leader's ability to protect those who follow them. Outsiders often call the Maw barbaric, but it is not lawless.

Feuds, challenges, raids, harsh hospitality, border diplomacy, and martial rivalry all operate inside customs and obligations that outsiders may fail to recognize. Alliances often depend more on personal obligation and proven conduct than on written institutions.

## What awaits in play

Use it for demon clan politics, feuds, warbands, challenge customs, harsh hospitality, raiding conflicts, border diplomacy, martial rivalries, and alliances built through personal obligation rather than written institutions.

These are possibilities rather than a required story path; a campaign may encounter the country through travel, diplomacy, conflict, trade, personal ties, or news from elsewhere in Eldaven.
