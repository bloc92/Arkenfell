# The Nightglass Principality

The Nightglass Principality is a small vampire-ruled domain of moonlit estates, mist towns, black-glass chapels, night gardens, oath villages, sealed crypts, blood courts, and carefully enforced hospitality.

## Blood Court Society

The Principality survives through law, etiquette, protected mortal populations, old wealth, and strict control over hunger. Mortals may live under charters, service contracts, village obligations, or house protection; their lives are constrained, but they are not automatically without rights or leverage.

Invitation, guest-right, feeding permission, lineage memory, debt, and insult matter deeply. Nightglass society is polite, cold, beautiful, dangerous, and old. Not every vampire is feral, and not every mortal is helpless.

## Religion

Its benefactor god is **Noctheris, the Crimson Guest**, patron of invitation, blood debt, restrained hunger, vampire etiquette, night courts, immortal memory, predatory courtesy, and hospitality with teeth.
