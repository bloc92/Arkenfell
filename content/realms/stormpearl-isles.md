# The Stormpearl Isles

The Stormpearl Isles are not a single centralized kingdom. Their political life is spread among island councils, free captains, pearl families, smugglers, sea-priests, wreck divers, weather witches, privateers, lighthouse families, and old island houses.

## Island Authority

Law is local and practical. A captain's oath, lighthouse warning, shrine taboo, wreck-right, harbor custom, or council judgment can matter more than a distant claim of sovereignty. Outside powers may claim influence, but local pilots, armed captains, councils, and families possess the knowledge and force required to make island decisions real.

## Religion

The Isles honor **Thalyra, the Pearl-Warden**, goddess of island councils, pearl houses, storm signs, lighthouse rites, fishing fleets, boat law, free captains, and weather-wise survival.
