# The Hollowmere Confederacy

The Hollowmere Confederacy is a marsh-and-ford polity whose authority is built around councils, causeways, ferry roads, bridge control, militias, toll and witness practice, and compromises that have to remain enforceable in difficult country.

## Confederacy Rule

Hollowmere Council Ford is a central political location. Markets, council business, records, causeway guards, and public works sit close together because transport, law, defense, and livelihood are difficult to separate in wet border country.

Confederate authority is practical rather than absolute. Agreements matter because councils, local houses, guards, ford militias, witnesses, and communities are willing and able to uphold them.

**Brennar Reedwatch** serves as a Confederate ford-marshal and commands ford militias at bridges and ferry roads.
