# The Solarian Theocracy

The Solarian Theocracy lies southeast of the central trade roads and is ruled by a High Synod of temple-lords rather than a royal dynasty.

Its cities and institutions are built around sanctuaries, pilgrimage roads, healing houses, doctrine, relic custody, and public religious service. The realm is smaller than the greatest kingdoms, but its priests, healers, sanctuaries, and temple alliances give it influence far beyond its borders.

Foreign rulers may seek Solarian approval or mediation during succession disputes, religious controversies, public unrest, and dangerous magical crises. This influence is significant, but it does not automatically give the Theocracy legal authority inside another sovereign realm.

## What awaits in play

Use it for pilgrimage arcs, Healing Arcana, holy trials, disputed miracles, legitimacy petitions, and kindness bound to authority.

These are possibilities rather than a required story path; a campaign may encounter the country through travel, diplomacy, conflict, trade, personal ties, or news from elsewhere in Eldaven.
