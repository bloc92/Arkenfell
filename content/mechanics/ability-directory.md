# Abilities of Arkenfell

Browse all **1,350 configured abilities** in Arkenfell. Search by name, description, required skill or trait, required rank, bonus, or cooldown.

Select any ability name to open its individual article, including its requirements, bonus, cooldown, and complete rules text.

Abilities are grouped alphabetically because their actual requirements are more useful as searchable information than an invented ability taxonomy.
