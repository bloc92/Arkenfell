# Rules and Play

Arkenfell uses several connected systems to describe what characters are capable of, how they develop, and how the world responds to their actions.

This section explains those systems in player-facing terms. Exact outcomes still depend on opposition, circumstances, equipment, injury, resources, relationships, and the fiction of the current scene.

## Core Character Systems

- [Attributes](#attributes) represent broad natural and developed aptitudes.
- [Resources](#resources) track expendable reserves such as Health, Mana, and Stamina.
- [Skills](#skills) represent learned competence in particular methods or fields.
- [Learning New Skills](#learning-new-skills) explains how a character can acquire a configured skill they did not start with.
- [Trait Catalog](#trait-catalog) covers backgrounds, aptitudes, training, equipment choices, and other defining character features.
- [Milestone Titles](#milestone-titles) summarizes the major levels of long-term character progression.

## Skills and Abilities

A skill is broad competence: Bows, Persuasion, Smithing, Arcane Lore, or a particular Arcana.

An ability is a specific technique or named action. Abilities may require a particular skill level, character level, attribute, resource, or trait. Raising a skill can therefore make stronger or more specialized abilities available without automatically granting every technique at once.

## Growth Through Play

Characters gain experience by acting in the world, overcoming meaningful challenges, discovering places, developing skills, and participating in the story.

Advancement can provide stronger skills, attributes, traits, resources, and abilities. Growth should reflect what the character actually does and experiences rather than appearing without a credible cause.

## Modes of Play

[Game Modes](#game-modes) allow a campaign to emphasize different structures, tones, pacing, and challenges. They shape how a story is presented without replacing the world's underlying canon.
