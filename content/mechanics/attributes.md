# Attributes

Arkenfell uses nine core attributes. Characters begin with a base value of **8** in each attribute and receive **20 starting attribute points**. The maximum starting value is **18**, while progression can raise attributes further.

| Attribute | Primary domain | Resource contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Ranged, aimed, and thrown attacks | +1 Stamina |
| Communication | Persuasion, deception, etiquette, bargaining, leadership, performance, verbal intimidation, rumors | +1 Stamina |
| Constitution | Endurance, exposure, poison, disease, pain | +2 Health |
| Dexterity | Stealth, acrobatics, locks, traps, legerdemain, riding, sailing, balance, precision | +1 Stamina |
| Fighting | Melee, brawling, dueling, parries, guards, shield pressure | +1 Health |
| Intelligence | Lore, research, appraisal, medicine, engineering, cartography, navigation, records, relic study | +1 Mana |
| Perception | Senses, tracking, searching, clues, danger, tells | +1 Mana |
| Strength | Lifting, climbing, jumping, grappling, forcing, carrying, mining, smithing, breaking | +1 Health |
| Willpower | Courage, discipline, fear resistance, pain focus, compulsion resistance, magical pressure, concentration, most direct Arcana | +1 Mana |

## Low Attribute Threshold

A value of **8 or lower** is treated as a low attribute for descriptive purposes.

- **Accuracy:** clumsy or poor at ranged attacks, precision aim, and fine-motor action under pressure.
- **Communication:** socially awkward, forgettable, blunt, or poor at projecting confidence and authority.
- **Constitution:** fragile, easily exhausted, or slow to recover from injury, poison, disease, or strain.
- **Dexterity:** slow-footed, clumsy, or poor at agility, balance, stealth, and fine physical control.
- **Fighting:** poorly trained in close combat, slow to read melee openings, or easily outmatched in weapon exchanges.
- **Intelligence:** struggles with theory, memory, tactics, technical learning, magic theory, or structured problem-solving.
- **Perception:** poor at noticing danger, reading subtle cues, tracking signs, or sensing hidden threats.
- **Strength:** physically weak and easily overpowered in direct contests of force.
- **Willpower:** easily shaken and poor at resisting fear, temptation, pain, magical pressure, or mental exhaustion.

## Combat Contributions

The configured system gives **Strength** and **Perception** damage modifiers of `0.5`, while **Dexterity** and **Constitution** contribute damage-reduction modifiers of `0.75` and `0.5` respectively. These are engine-facing values but are included here because they are part of the public character rules.
