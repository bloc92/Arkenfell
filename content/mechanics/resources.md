# Resources

Arkenfell uses persistent resources to represent bodily condition, magical reserve, physical exertion, and certain creature-specific needs.

| Resource | Base maximum | Gain per level | Passive recharge |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Health | 45 | +3 | 0 |
| Mana | 10 | +2 | +1 |
| Stamina | 40 | +2 | 0 during exertion |
| Blood | Dynamic vampire resource; normally begins at 0 unless applicable | +2 | -1 rather than natural recovery |

## Health

Health represents the character's ability to remain physically functional through injury. The configured baseline is **45 Health**, increasing by **3 per level** before other modifiers.

Health should change because actual harm or recovery occurs, not merely because the story mentions pain or an already-existing wound. Serious injury and story consequences can matter even when a character remains above zero Health.

## Mana

Mana is inner magical energy used to express Arcana, cast spells, power rituals and supernatural techniques, sustain effects, and overchannel magic.

Typical configured cost bands are:

- trivial effect: **0–1 Mana**
- minor effect: **1–3 Mana**
- moderate spell or combat technique: **3–6 Mana**
- major spell, strong defense, or area effect: **6–12 Mana**
- Rare Arcana: normally **+5–10 Mana** above a comparable standard effect
- limited Legendary effect: normally **12–20 Mana** plus meaningful strain or consequence
- ritual-scale Legendary effect: **50+ Mana equivalent** plus preparation, support, or story-scale requirements

At **0 Mana**, magic may be fueled with Stamina at a **3 Stamina : 1 Mana-equivalent** exchange when the action otherwise permits it.

## Stamina

Stamina represents active physical exertion: combat maneuvers, sprinting, hard climbing, swimming, sustained labor, desperate movement, heavy physical techniques, and other meaningful strain.

The baseline is **40 Stamina**. It does not passively recover while the character remains under active exertion. Ordinary conversation, standing, walking normally, reading, thinking, resting, and passive tension do not consume Stamina merely because a scene is stressful.

Recovery comes from an actual pause, meal, rest, sleep, treatment, potion, recovery ability, or other supported opportunity to stop exerting.

## Blood

Blood is a vampire-specific resource rather than a universal character resource. It does **not** regenerate through ordinary rest. Feeding on living creatures can restore Blood to **50**.

Configured hunger bands are:

- **30+ — Sated**
- **15–29 — Hungry**
- **5–14 — Starving**
- **0–4 — Critical**

Below 5 Blood, control becomes dangerously unstable. Blood starvation does not automatically kill a vampire; it threatens reason and self-control. A healthy blood level supports blood magic, while feeding and its moral/legal circumstances remain part of the fiction rather than a free refill button.
