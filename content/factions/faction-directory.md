# Faction Directory

This directory lists established Arkenfell factions and political organizations that appear in the current World data. A faction's presence in the directory does not mean every character knows its private motives, membership, or secrets.

## Valenreach and Crown-Adjacent Organizations

### The Crownspire Collegium
A major Valenreach magical institution associated with classification, training, discipline, records, experimental caution, student hierarchy, restricted knowledge, and Crown-facing magical service.

### The Lantern Synod
A significant religious and political institution within Valenreach. Its spiritual and social influence does not grant it automatic unilateral authority over every Crown or succession matter.

### The Argent Ledger
An established organization active in the Crownlake Heartlands and Valenreach's political-economic environment.

### The Gilded Knives
An established faction with activity in and around the Crownlake Heartlands.

### The Ashen Veil
An established covert faction with important historical relevance. Public knowledge of specific operations remains limited; sealed campaign truths are not included here.

### The Ashbound Wardens
An established Valenreach-linked faction whose public identity should remain consistent with its canonical faction record.

### The Cinder Gate Cults
An established faction associated with demon-related religious or cult activity. Its canon should use the current uncivilized/barbaric demon framing where applicable rather than generic infernal-contract assumptions.

### House Valmere
A wealthy western Valenreach ducal house whose influence rests on fertile valleys, river access, routes, harvest contracts, retainers, warehouses, marriage politics, and usefulness to the Crown. Lady Selira Valmere is publicly betrothed to Crown Prince Rowan.

### High Elven Noble Houses
An established collective of high-elven noble houses with political and magical-policy interests extending into Valenreach court life.

## Regional and Independent Factions

### The Stormpearl Free Captains
Ship-lords, privateers, smugglers, lighthouse families, pearl commanders, island envoys, and storm navigators whose power comes from ships, weather knowledge, reefs, crews, and harbor influence. They are not automatically pirates.

### The Stormscale Conclave
A loose alliance of white and air dragons associated with high-altitude territories, storm passes, aeries, wind routes, mountain skies, old territorial memory, and sky-oaths. The Conclave is not a mortal nation and does not directly rule ordinary settlements by default.

### The Umbral Brood
A legendary shadow-dragon grouping believed extinct in ordinary public understanding. It survives primarily in warning myths connected with darkness, memory, hidden flame, soul-silence, Greyhaven, and forgotten pact lines.

### The Rootbound Moot
An established woodland collective associated with dryads, groves, old nature ties, and the Deepgreen Wilds.

### The Verdant Circle
An established nature-linked organization active in the Deepgreen Wilds and nearby regions.

### The Blackwater Caravans
An established mobile trade/travel faction active across several difficult western and southern routes.

### The Red Salt Brotherhood
An established smuggling or maritime faction appearing around Stormpearl and western sea routes.

### The Freefang Captains
An independent road and travel faction associated with mobile captains and frontier movement.

### The Broken Crown Claimants
A claimant faction centered on surviving royal legitimacy, grievance, inheritance arguments, and the memory of a fallen crown.

### The Mourning Bell Wardens
A warden organization based around Mourning Bell Bastion, patrol duty, bells, and protective military service.

### Eldmark Lawspeaker Circles
Custodians and practitioners of Eldmark's remembered law and public legal tradition.

### Eldmark Beacon Wardens
Eldmark defenders and route-watchers associated with beacon networks, coast and river warnings, and military readiness.

### Eldmark Barrow-Keepers
Custodians of ancestral barrows, war-dead memory, and associated traditions.
