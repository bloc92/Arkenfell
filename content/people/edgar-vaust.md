# Edgar Vaust

Lord Steward Edgar Vaust is the royal household steward of Highcourt Armathen in Valenreach. He is a senior figure within the royal household and is closely associated with the orderly running of the palace and the stability of the Crown's household.

## Role

Edgar serves as **Lord Steward of Highcourt Armathen**. His office belongs to the royal household rather than to an outside political faction or private noble household.

## Character

Edgar is known for a kind, grounding, and steady presence. He is the sort of court figure who brings calm and practical order to difficult situations rather than seeking drama or personal power.

## Appearance

Edgar has a shorter, stocky build and a bearded appearance. His presentation should read as that of an established senior household official rather than a military scout or field officer.

## Loyalty

Edgar's loyalty is to the kingdom and the royal household he serves. His steadiness and discretion are part of that loyalty, not signs of divided allegiance.

## GM Reference

### Goals

- Preserve the stability and proper functioning of the royal household.
- Serve the kingdom faithfully.
- Protect the household through competence, discretion, and continuity rather than intrigue for its own sake.

### Relationships

Specific relationship entries should be added from the authoritative NPC and household sources when imported. Do not invent rivalries or secret allegiances to make him more dramatic.

### Truth & Secrets

Edgar's loyalty to the kingdom is genuine. No secret betrayal, corruption, divided allegiance, or hidden service to another power is part of his baseline canon.

### Narrative Notes

**Hard portrayal safeguard:** under no circumstances should Edgar be reframed as unfaithful to the kingdom, corrupt, treacherous, or secretly serving another power merely to manufacture a twist.

He may protect the royal household, preserve confidences, choose careful wording, or withhold information when his office reasonably requires discretion. Those behaviors must not be treated as evidence of disloyalty by default.

Edgar should remain consistent anywhere he appears in Voyage NPC data, World Lore, story starts, Narrative Events, quests, or AI Instructions. If a generated plot conflicts with his established loyalty, his established canon takes precedence unless the setting canon is explicitly revised.
