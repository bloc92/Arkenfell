# Important NPC Directory

This directory collects authored people important enough to remain useful across campaigns, politics, factions, or major locations. It uses public-facing information only; hidden motives and unrevealed secrets are kept in the private GM canon.

## Valenreach

### King Aldric
King of Valenreach and head of the royal household at Highcourt Armathen.

### Queen Ceralie
Queen of Valenreach and an elite healer within the royal household.

### Crown Prince Rowan
Public heir to the Valenreach Crown.

### Princess Elira
Princess of Valenreach and younger royal child of Aldric and Ceralie.

### Edgar Vaust
Lord Steward of Highcourt Armathen and a steady senior figure of the royal household. [Full article](#edgar-vaust)

### Duke Arvelion Valmere
Head of House Valmere and Duke of the Western Vale Routes. A calculating, disciplined house patriarch focused on the long-term position of his family.

### Luceran Valmere
Son and heir of Duke Arvelion Valmere.

### Lady Selira Valmere
Daughter of Duke Arvelion Valmere and publicly betrothed to Crown Prince Rowan.

### Dravros
A dwarven member of the Royal Duel Corps of Armathen, known for controlled duel-field training, practical defense, and an unusually patient approach to weapons and technique.

### Caelion Ilsem
A lord among the High Elven Noble Houses who represents high-elven interests in magical policy discussions at Highcourt Armathen.

## Arcanum Ascendancy

### Archon Selvaris Tyth
High Archon of the Arcanum Ascendancy, a senior human mage and public head of the Ascendancy Conclave. His office oversees magical classification, research access, licenses, and containment policy.

## Dreadmark Dominion

### King Kael Malrec
King of the Dreadmark Dominion.

### Crown Prince Veyron Malrec
Heir to the Dreadmark Dominion.

### Princess Vaelyra Malrec
Daughter of King Kael Malrec and younger sister of Crown Prince Veyron. She is trained in Air Arcana and belongs to the upper royal family of the Dominion.

## Emberhorn Accord

### Iravelle Cinderhorn
Contract Sovereign of the Emberhorn Accord, a senior tiefling ruler associated with Fire Arcana, contract magic, and Sovereign Arcana.

### Varos Cinderhorn
Consort and member of the ruling Cinderhorn family.

### Cindrel Cinderhorn
Heir of the Emberhorn Accord and a citizen-militia commander. Elder child of Iravelle and Varos and sibling of Zarelle; associated with Fire and Heroic Arcana.

### Zarelle Cinderhorn
Member of the ruling Cinderhorn family and sibling of Cindrel.

## Coral Throne

### Thalassira Pearl-Crown
Senior ruler of the Coral Throne and a major figure of its pearl-and-tide court.

### Nerion Pearl-Crown
Tide-consort and reef-law interpreter; husband of Thalassira and father of Merynth and Syrava. Associated with Water Arcana and tide law.

### Merynth Pearl-Crown
Pearl-heir and lagoon diplomat, elder child of Thalassira and Nerion and sibling of Syrava. Associated with Water and Moon Arcana.

### Syrava Pearl-Crown
Member of the Pearl-Crown ruling family and sibling of Merynth.

## Stormpearl Isles

### Nessa Stormmere
Heir-apparent of Stormpearl Haven and a storm-road captain, daughter of Alyr and Marisel and part of the upper leadership of the Stormpearl Free Captains.

## Fangbound Clans

### Torgul Stormhoof
Young Stormhoof clan-heir and champion, associated with Heroic Arcana and the leadership of the Fangbound Clans.

### Ashka Stormhoof
Senior Stormhoof figure associated with the Great Fang Moot and Fangbound leadership.

## Ironroot Holds

### Brunna Anvilroot
High Thane of the Ironroot Holds. A dwarven matriarch whose authority gives the federation a visible face for diplomacy, succession, oathcraft, and internal clan pressure. Associated with Earth and Rune Arcana.

### Dorrik Anvilroot
Forge-consort and oath-husband of Brunna Anvilroot, master smith, father of Berra, and one of the few people permitted to contradict the High Thane openly before the clan benches. Associated with Rune Arcana and forge rites.

### Berra Anvilroot
Shield-heir of Kar Anvilroot, daughter of Brunna and Dorrik. A disciplined commander associated with Earth Arcana and shield-rune practice.

### Torvek Anvilroot
Deep-road auditor and cousin of the High Thane. He audits tunnel rights, road debts, mine claims, and passages important to the Holds. Associated with Earth Arcana and survey wards.

## Cloudspire Dominion

### Aereth Cloudspire
Senior Cloudspire ruler and central figure of the Dominion's ruling household.

### Thalen Windmere
Sky-consort and route-law keeper of Cloudspire; husband of Aereth and father of Syll and Kendra. He oversees landing rights, storm-route charters, and marriage alliances between cliff houses.

### Syll Cloudspire
Member of the Cloudspire ruling family and child of Aereth and Thalen.

### Kendra Cloudspire
Young member of the Cloudspire ruling family, associated with Air Arcana.

## Nightglass Principality

### Vespera Nightglass
Senior ruler of the Nightglass Principality and head of its ruling line.

### Morvane Nightglass
Prince-consort and keeper of old blood-contract traditions; consort of Vespera and father of Evelisse. Associated with Moon Arcana and blood rites.

### Evelisse Nightglass
Recognized heir of Vespera and Morvane, associated with Moon and Enchantment Arcana.

### Sable Nightglass
Black-coach marshal and cousin of the ruling line. She commands roads, escorts, and curfew enforcement and belongs to the upper leadership of the Principality.

## Eldmark Kingdom

### Hroldan Lawstone
King of Eldmark and father of Eydren and Signe.

### Jora Lawstone
Queen of Eldmark and mother of Eydren and Signe.

### Crown Prince Eydren Lawstone
Heir to Eldmark and a beacon-war captain, trained among beacon wardens and river household warriors. Associated with Lightning Arcana.

### Signe Lawstone
Younger child of Hroldan and Jora and sister of Eydren.

## Hollowmere Confederacy

### Marwen Fenbridge
Senior political leader of the Hollowmere Confederacy and cousin of Brennar Reedwatch.

### Brennar Reedwatch
Confederate ford-marshal and commander of ford militias, responsible for making Hollowmere agreements enforceable at bridges and ferry roads.

### Oriel Fenbridge
Council courier-heir of House Fenbridge and younger member of Hollowmere's political network.

## Palegrave Crown

### Yska Icecrown
Frost-heir and war-daughter of the Palegrave Crown, an elite figure associated with Cold and Earth Arcana.

### Orun Cairn-Tongue
Cairn-law speaker and keeper of old genealogies, treaty names, burial rights, and inherited claims.

## Independent and Regional Figures

### Nara Freefang
Road Queen of the Freefang Captains, a strong mobile leader associated with Heroic and Air Arcana.

### Ysmera Ashcrown
Dowager claimant-mother of House Ashcrown and a senior figure of the Broken Crown Claimants, carrying the memory and pressure of an older fallen crown.

### Moss-Under-Stars
A dryad root-spouse and moon-grove speaker associated with the Rootbound Moot.

### Tovin Blackbell
Deputy bell-captain and nephew of Marshal Maervyn within the Mourning Bell Wardens.

NPCs marked unknown or deliberately concealed in Voyage are not promoted into this public directory merely because their records exist.
