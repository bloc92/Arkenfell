# Countries of Eldaven

Arkenfell is the single realm represented by this world. Eldaven is a continent within Arkenfell, and its kingdoms, empires, dominions, republics, theocracies, accords, holds, and confederacies are countries or sovereign political powers—not separate realms.

This directory contains the established countries currently represented in the Arkenfell data. Use the search and group controls to browse them, then open a linked country for its full article.
