# Elementborn — Galeborn

Elementborn are mortal people whose ancestry carries a persistent elemental expression strong enough to shape physiology and magical affinity. Elementborn are not elementals, and their element does not determine culture, morality, profession, or personality. Galeborn carry air-linked traits such as unusually light movement, hair or clothing that reacts readily to small currents, pale cloudlike markings, or an instinctive sense for changes in airflow.

## In play

Your body favors light, controlled movement and carries Air affinity. This does not grant flight or immunity to falling without another established capability.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Acrobatics +8, Seeing +8, Air Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
