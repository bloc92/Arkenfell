# Dhampir

Dhampirs are living mortals with partial vampiric ancestry or a stable vampire-touched bloodline. They may have cool skin, subtle fangs, unusual eyes, muted pulse, and pronounced nocturnal senses, but they are not Vampires and do not automatically possess the Beast, blood dependence, immortality, sunlight burning, invitation restrictions, missing reflections, or the Vampire subsystem.

## In play

You possess restrained vampiric sensory inheritance while remaining a living mortal. Becoming an actual Vampire is a separate supernatural transformation and subsystem that this trait neither activates nor requires.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Seeing +11, Hearing +5, Smelling +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
