# Lynxfolk Beastkin

Lynxfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus tufted lynx ears, short tail, pale or spotted markings, silent steps, and watchful eyes. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Tufted ears, sharp low-light sight, and quiet movement favor stealth and sensory awareness. Do not force solitary, aloof, predatory, or hunter behavior.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Stealth +8, Seeing +8, Hearing +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
