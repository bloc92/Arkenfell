# Werewolf Beastkin

Werewolf Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus wolf ears, tail, sharp senses, strong build, and a more feral edge in posture, voice, or movement. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Sharpened wolf-like senses, strong build, and a visibly feral edge make trails, nearby movement, and physical pressure easier to read. This trait does not by itself grant shapeshifting, uncontrollable rage, lunar transformation, or lycanthropic powers.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tracking +11, Tolerance +5, Intimidation +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
