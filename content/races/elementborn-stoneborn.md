# Elementborn — Stoneborn

Elementborn are mortal people whose ancestry carries a persistent elemental expression strong enough to shape physiology and magical affinity. Elementborn are not elementals, and their element does not determine culture, morality, profession, or personality. Stoneborn carry earth-linked traits such as dense musculature, mineral flecks, stone-toned patches, heavy bones, or a grounded physical presence.

## In play

Your body is unusually dense and carries an Earth affinity. This does not make you literally stone, immovable, or immune to ordinary injury.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Might +8, Tolerance +8, Earth Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
