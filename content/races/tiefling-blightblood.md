# Tiefling — Blightblood

Blightblood tieflings carry demonic ancestry associated with venom, corruption, caustic environments, diseased places, and toxic creatures. They may show unusual skin tones, dark veins, sharp teeth, strange scents, or warning-like markings. They are not inherently diseased, poisonous to touch, unclean, or malicious.

## In play

Your ancestry gives strong tolerance for bodily intrusion, keen chemical scent awareness, and Poison affinity. It does not make your body automatically venomous or grant poison attacks without another mechanic.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Smelling +8, Poison Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
