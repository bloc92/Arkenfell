# Hawkfolk Beastkin

Hawkfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus sharp eyes, featherlike hair or markings, small feather accents, light posture, and talonlike nails. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Exceptional distance vision and rapid visual tracking make hawkfolk especially good at spotting movement, reading terrain from high ground, and maintaining direction across open country. Do not imply flight unless another trait or ability grants it.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Seeing +11, Tracking +5, Navigation +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
