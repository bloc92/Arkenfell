# Raccoonfolk Beastkin

Raccoonfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus raccoon ears, a ringed tail, mask-like markings, quick fingers, and alert posture. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Sensitive hands, quick fingers, sharp close attention, and strong tactile awareness favor searching and delicate manipulation. Theft, scavenging, and criminality are not inherent racial behaviors.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Searching +8, Touching +8, Legerdemain +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
