# Wolfkin Beastkin

Wolfkin Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus wolf ears, a wolf tail, sharp eyes or slightly sharper human teeth, and controlled watchful body language. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Keen scent and hearing make trails, nearby movement, and recent passage unusually noticeable. Pack loyalty, empathy, aggression, hierarchy, and wilderness culture are not biologically mandatory.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Smelling +8, Hearing +8, Tracking +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
