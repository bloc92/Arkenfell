# Caelari — Mercyborn

Caelari (pronounced kay-LAR-ee) are mortal people whose ancestry carries an old celestial influence. They are not angels, chosen servants of a deity, automatically virtuous, or proof of divine favor. Mercyborn Caelari express a restorative celestial inheritance often visible as warm skin, gentle luminescence, unusually steady vital signs, or markings associated with renewal.

## In play

Your celestial inheritance is associated with restoration and Healing affinity. It does not make you compassionate, holy, medically trained, or able to heal beyond your actual Arcana skill and abilities.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Empathy +8, Tolerance +8, Healing Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
