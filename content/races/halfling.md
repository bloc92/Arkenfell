# Halfling

Halflings are small, resilient mortal humanoids with compact builds, light movement, and proportions that let them pass through crowded or larger-built environments easily. Halfling ancestry does not determine hospitality, cooking, farming, trade, criminality, or any particular culture.

## In play

Your compact size, balance, and resilient body favor unobtrusive movement and agility. Treat household customs, food traditions, professions, and social behavior as cultural or personal rather than racial.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Stealth +8, Acrobatics +8, Tolerance +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
