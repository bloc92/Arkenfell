# Serpentfolk Beastkin

Serpentfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus slit pupils, subtle scale-pattern markings, smooth movement, cool skin tone, and snake-like eyes. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Scent-sensitive perception, still posture, smooth movement, and tactile awareness favor quiet observation and unobtrusive movement. Serpentfolk are not inherently deceitful, cold, manipulative, or malicious.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Smelling +8, Stealth +8, Touching +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
