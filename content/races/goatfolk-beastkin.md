# Goatfolk Beastkin

Goatfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus goat horns, goat ears, sturdy human feet or practical mountain footwear, and sure-footed movement. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Sure-footed balance and strong climbing instincts make steep slopes, broken ground, narrow ledges, and vertical movement unusually natural. Stubbornness is not an enforced personality trait.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Climbing +8, Acrobatics +8, Jumping +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
