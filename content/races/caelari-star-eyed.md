# Caelari — Star-Eyed

Caelari (pronounced kay-LAR-ee) are mortal people whose ancestry carries an old celestial influence. They are not angels, chosen servants of a deity, automatically virtuous, or proof of divine favor. Star-Eyed Caelari express an inheritance associated with distant light, signs, patterns, celestial observation, and unusual perception. Their eyes may resemble stars, pale fire, deep night, or shifting points of light.

## In play

Your celestial inheritance carries Divination affinity and unusually focused perception. It does not grant prophecy, secret knowledge, destiny, or access to information your actual abilities cannot reveal.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Seeing +8, Self-Discipline +8, Divination Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
