# Elementborn — Rootborn

Elementborn are mortal people whose ancestry carries a persistent elemental expression strong enough to shape physiology and magical affinity. Elementborn are not elementals, and their element does not determine culture, morality, profession, or personality. Rootborn carry wood- and living-growth traits such as barklike patches, leaf or vine markings, earthy scents, unusual hair textures, or subtle seasonal changes in coloration.

## In play

Your physiology carries Wood affinity and heightened organic scent awareness. You remain a mortal person rather than a plant creature and do not automatically photosynthesize, root, or regenerate.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Smelling +8, Tolerance +8, Wood Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
