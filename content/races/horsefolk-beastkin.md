# Horsefolk Beastkin

Horsefolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus horse ears, a horse tail, strong legs, alert posture, and road-hardened endurance. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Strong legs, road-hardened endurance, and a powerful humanlike frame favor sustained running and physical exertion. Horsefolk are human-bodied beastkin; do not treat them as mounts or assume riding or navigation skill from ancestry.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Running +8, Tolerance +8, Might +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
