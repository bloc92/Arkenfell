# Elementborn — Tideborn

Elementborn are mortal people whose ancestry carries a persistent elemental expression strong enough to shape physiology and magical affinity. Elementborn are not elementals, and their element does not determine culture, morality, profession, or personality. Tideborn carry water-linked traits such as cool skin, subtle webbing, aquatic coloration, unusual comfort in water, or eyes resembling deep water.

## In play

Your physiology is unusually comfortable in water and carries Water affinity. This does not grant underwater breathing unless another mechanic explicitly does so.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Swimming +8, Tolerance +8, Water Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
