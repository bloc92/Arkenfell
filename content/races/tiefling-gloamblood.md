# Tiefling — Gloamblood

Gloamblood tieflings express demonic ancestry tied to darkness, concealment, muted presence, and places where light fails. They may have very dark horns, low-light adapted eyes, subdued coloration, or shadows that seem deeper around their silhouette without becoming supernatural by default.

## In play

Your infernal ancestry favors dim-light perception, unobtrusive movement, and Shadow affinity. It does not make you secretive, criminal, sinister, or automatically capable of forbidden Shadow feats.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Stealth +8, Seeing +8, Shadow Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
