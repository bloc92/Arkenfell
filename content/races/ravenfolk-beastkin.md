# Ravenfolk Beastkin

Ravenfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus dark featherlike hair or markings, raven-dark eyes, possible winglike clothing patterns, and quick expressive movement. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Sharp sight and hearing support close observation and noticing changed details. Omens, cleverness, empathy, and mystical interpretation may exist in culture or personality but are not imposed by ancestry.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Seeing +8, Hearing +8, Searching +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
