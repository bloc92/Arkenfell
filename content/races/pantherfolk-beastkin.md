# Pantherfolk Beastkin

Pantherfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus panther ears, a tail, dark hair or markings, low smooth movement, and eyes that catch dim light. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Low, smooth movement, strong balance, and eyes adapted to dim light make pantherfolk exceptionally suited to moving quietly and following prey or threats. Do not impose a violent or solitary temperament.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Stealth +11, Acrobatics +5, Seeing +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
