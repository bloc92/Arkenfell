# Human — Tempered

Humans possess unusually plastic physical, mental, social, and magical development, a quality often called Adaptable Potential. Rather than expressing one strongly specialized inherited form, human potential responds exceptionally well to environment, experience, training, hardship, and opportunity. This selection describes how that potential developed before play; it is not a human subspecies, ethnicity, culture, profession, or personality type. Your development settled most strongly into a durable, conditioned body able to apply force and keep functioning under physical strain.

## In play

You are Human. Your Adaptable Potential expressed through physical conditioning, resilience, and controlled exertion; other humans may develop very differently.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Might +8, Self-Discipline +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
