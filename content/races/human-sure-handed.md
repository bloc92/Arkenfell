# Human — Sure-Handed

Humans possess unusually plastic physical, mental, social, and magical development, a quality often called Adaptable Potential. Rather than expressing one strongly specialized inherited form, human potential responds exceptionally well to environment, experience, training, hardship, and opportunity. This selection describes how that potential developed before play; it is not a human subspecies, ethnicity, culture, profession, or personality type. Your development favored unusually precise touch, fine hand control, delicate manipulation, and quick coordination.

## In play

You are Human. Your Adaptable Potential expressed through fine coordination and tactile control; it does not imply criminality, stage training, or any particular profession.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Touching +8, Legerdemain +8, Acrobatics +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
