# Lizardfolk

Lizardfolk are fully reptilian humanoids with scales, reptilian heads and eyes, tails, claws, and strong jaws. Different lineages may resemble monitors, iguanas, crocodilians, geckos, desert lizards, or other reptiles while retaining an upright humanoid body plan compatible with ordinary equipment. Lizardfolk are natural mortal people and have no inherent connection to dragons.

## In play

Scales, reptilian sensory biology, and a durable mortal body support environmental tolerance, scent-based awareness, and sharp observation. Lizardfolk are not inherently primitive, emotionless, draconic, or tied to wetlands.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Smelling +8, Seeing +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
