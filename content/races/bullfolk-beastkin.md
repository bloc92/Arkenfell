# Bullfolk Beastkin

Bullfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus bull horns, bovine ears, a tail, broad build, thick neck, and heavy presence. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

A broad frame, thick neck, horns, and heavy presence make raw force and physical pressure especially convincing. Their imposing appearance can affect first impressions without forcing a domineering personality.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Might +8, Tolerance +8, Intimidation +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
