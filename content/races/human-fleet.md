# Human — Fleet

Humans possess unusually plastic physical, mental, social, and magical development, a quality often called Adaptable Potential. Rather than expressing one strongly specialized inherited form, human potential responds exceptionally well to environment, experience, training, hardship, and opportunity. This selection describes how that potential developed before play; it is not a human subspecies, ethnicity, culture, profession, or personality type. Your development favored speed, balance, acceleration, and rapid adjustment to movement.

## In play

You are Human. Your Adaptable Potential expressed through quick movement, balance, and visual reaction rather than a separate bloodline or culture.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Acrobatics +8, Running +8, Seeing +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
