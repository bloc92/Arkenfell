# Batfolk Beastkin

Batfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus bat ears, dark eyes, sharp hearing, and night-adapted posture. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Exceptional hearing and comfort in darkness make faint movement, echoes, and nearby activity easier to notice and follow. Bright, exposed places may be less comfortable, but do not force fearfulness, nocturnal habits, or a particular personality.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Hearing +11, Navigation +5, Stealth +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
