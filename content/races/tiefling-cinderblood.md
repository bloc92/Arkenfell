# Tiefling — Cinderblood

Cinderblood tieflings express demonic ancestry associated with heat, embers, furnaces, and flame. They remain fundamentally mortal people and may show swept horns, ember-bright eyes, unusually warm skin, soot-dark markings, or faint heat around the hands under stress. Their ancestry does not determine culture, morality, profession, or allegiance.

## In play

Your infernal ancestry is visibly and magically heat-linked. Fire affinity and an imposing infernal presence are inherited traits, not proof of cruelty, aggression, or loyalty to demons.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Intimidation +8, Fire Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
