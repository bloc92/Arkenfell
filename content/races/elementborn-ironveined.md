# Elementborn — Ironveined

Elementborn are mortal people whose ancestry carries a persistent elemental expression strong enough to shape physiology and magical affinity. Elementborn are not elementals, and their element does not determine culture, morality, profession, or personality. Ironveined carry metal-linked traits such as metallic flecks in skin or eyes, unusual bodily density, mineral-colored nails, or heightened tactile awareness of worked metal and rigid materials.

## In play

Your body carries a stable Metal affinity and unusually strong tactile response to dense materials. You are not literally made of metal and gain no automatic armor.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Touching +8, Tolerance +8, Metal Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
