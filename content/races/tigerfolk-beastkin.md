# Tigerfolk Beastkin

Tigerfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus tiger ears, a tail, striped skin or hair markings, bright eyes, strong build, and fluid heavy movement. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

A strong build, fluid heavy movement, striped markings, sharp sight, and quiet footwork favor strength and stealth. Courage, aggression, dominance, and hunting behavior remain personal or learned rather than racial.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Might +8, Stealth +8, Seeing +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
