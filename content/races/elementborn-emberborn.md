# Elementborn — Emberborn

Elementborn are mortal people whose ancestry carries a persistent elemental expression strong enough to shape physiology and magical affinity. Elementborn are not elementals, and their element does not determine culture, morality, profession, or personality. Emberborn carry fire-linked traits such as unusual body heat, emberlike eyes, warm markings, soot-dark hair tones, or skin that handles heat unusually well.

## In play

Your body and magic carry a stable Fire affinity. Heat-linked traits are inherited, but you do not automatically ignite objects, resist all fire, or possess fire abilities you have not learned.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Courage +8, Fire Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
