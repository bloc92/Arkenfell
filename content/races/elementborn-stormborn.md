# Elementborn — Stormborn

Elementborn are mortal people whose ancestry carries a persistent elemental expression strong enough to shape physiology and magical affinity. Elementborn are not elementals, and their element does not determine culture, morality, profession, or personality. Stormborn carry lightning-linked traits such as static in the hair, bright irises, branching markings, tingling skin, or visible electrical reactions under stress.

## In play

Your body and magic carry Lightning affinity and respond strongly to sudden exertion. You do not automatically discharge electricity or resist lightning without an explicit mechanic.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Running +8, Courage +8, Lightning Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
