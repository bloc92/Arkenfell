# Otterfolk Beastkin

Otterfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus otter ears, an otter tail, expressive face, water-ready posture, and quick playful movement. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

A water-ready body, strong swimming instincts, and agile movement make currents and aquatic movement unusually natural. Playfulness and sociability are not required personality traits.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Swimming +11, Acrobatics +5, Tolerance +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
