# Races of Arkenfell

Browse all **69 playable Race traits** currently configured in public Arkenfell. Each entry links to a player-facing article and records the exact mechanical benefits granted by the Race trait.

Race describes ancestry, inherited physiology, or a supernatural lineage. It does **not** determine culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, personality, or the choices a character makes. Those parts of a character come from their life and from the player.

Several related peoples appear as families of choices—particularly Beastkin, Caelari, Elementborn, Humans, and Tieflings—because their configured lineages have meaningfully different inherited traits.
