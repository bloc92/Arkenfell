# Elementborn — Frostborn

Elementborn are mortal people whose ancestry carries a persistent elemental expression strong enough to shape physiology and magical affinity. Elementborn are not elementals, and their element does not determine culture, morality, profession, or personality. Frostborn carry cold-linked traits such as low body temperature, frostlike markings, pale or crystalline coloration, and exceptional comfort in severe cold.

## In play

Your physiology is exceptionally adapted to temperature stress and carries Cold affinity. Cold skin and temperature sensitivity do not make you immune to freezing or harmful heat.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +11, Touching +5, Cold Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
