# Human — Worldwise

Humans possess unusually plastic physical, mental, social, and magical development, a quality often called Adaptable Potential. Rather than expressing one strongly specialized inherited form, human potential responds exceptionally well to environment, experience, training, hardship, and opportunity. This selection describes how that potential developed before play; it is not a human subspecies, ethnicity, culture, profession, or personality type. Your development responded most strongly to living among people, changing expectations, and social environments, making you unusually good at reading and adapting to others.

## In play

You are Human. Your Adaptable Potential expressed socially: you read people and unfamiliar social situations well, without this trait granting culture, rank, authority, or automatic charm.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Empathy +8, Social Inquiry +8, Persuasion +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
