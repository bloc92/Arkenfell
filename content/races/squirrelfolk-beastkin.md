# Squirrelfolk Beastkin

Squirrelfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus squirrel ears, a large fluffy tail, quick feet, bright eyes, and agile hands. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Strong climbing instincts, fast balance corrections, quick hands, and a large expressive tail favor vertical movement and delicate manipulation. Hoarding or nervousness may be habits, never mandatory racial behavior.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Climbing +8, Acrobatics +8, Legerdemain +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
