# Human — Keen-Sensed

Humans possess unusually plastic physical, mental, social, and magical development, a quality often called Adaptable Potential. Rather than expressing one strongly specialized inherited form, human potential responds exceptionally well to environment, experience, training, hardship, and opportunity. This selection describes how that potential developed before play; it is not a human subspecies, ethnicity, culture, profession, or personality type. Repeated use and environmental pressure sharpened your ordinary human senses into unusually dependable awareness.

## In play

You are Human. Your Adaptable Potential expressed through trained sensory awareness; this is heightened development, not supernatural perception.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Seeing +8, Hearing +8, Searching +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
