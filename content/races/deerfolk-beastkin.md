# Deerfolk Beastkin

Deerfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus deer ears, soft eyes, possible antlers, light steps, and woodland poise. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Long-range hearing, wide visual awareness, light movement, and strong running ability favor early detection and rapid movement. Do not impose timidity, gentleness, woodland culture, or tracking training as racial behavior.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Running +8, Hearing +8, Seeing +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
