# Caelari — Dawnmarked

Caelari (pronounced kay-LAR-ee) are mortal people whose ancestry carries an old celestial influence. They are not angels, chosen servants of a deity, automatically virtuous, or proof of divine favor. Dawnmarked Caelari express that inheritance through radiance, luminous or reflective eyes, bright markings, and an instinctive affinity for light.

## In play

Your celestial ancestry expresses through radiance and Light affinity. It can affect appearance and magical potential without determining faith, morality, destiny, or allegiance.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Seeing +8, Courage +8, Light Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
