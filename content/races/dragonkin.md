# Dragonkin

Dragonkin look fundamentally human but carry prominent draconic traits. They retain a fully humanlike face, skin, hands, feet, legs, and body plan while normally possessing horns, a scaled tail, and draconic wings, sometimes accompanied by slit pupils or small scale patches. They are not full humanoid dragons and should never be given reptilian heads, muzzles, full-body scales, digitigrade monster legs, or Dragonfolk anatomy.

## In play

Your horns, scaled tail, and wings are obvious draconic features on an otherwise human body; the wings aid balance, gliding, controlled descent, and plausible aerial movement, but unrestricted sustained flight requires an explicitly established ability or capability.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Acrobatics +8, Seeing +8, Intimidation +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
