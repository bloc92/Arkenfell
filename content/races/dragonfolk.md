# Dragonfolk

Dragonfolk are full-sized draconic humanoids with scaled bodies, distinctly draconic faces, horns, claws, tails, and powerful humanoid builds. They are mortal people rather than lesser dragons and can use ordinary humanoid clothing, armor, weapons, and equipment with practical adjustments for their horns and tails. Wings, breath weapons, and supernatural elemental powers are not universal Dragonfolk traits.

## In play

Your scales, horns, tail, and powerful draconic frame are always physically present; you naturally account for horn clearance, tail balance, scale coverage, and the space your body occupies without gaining automatic breath weapons, wings, or elemental powers.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Might +8, Intimidation +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
