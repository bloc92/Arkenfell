# Rabbitfolk Beastkin

Rabbitfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus long rabbit ears, expressive posture, quick movement, and compact athletic build. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Long ears, quick acceleration, responsive balance, and alert hearing favor rapid movement and reactions. Nervousness and timidity are not mandatory personality traits.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Running +8, Hearing +8, Acrobatics +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
