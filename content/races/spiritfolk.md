# Spiritfolk

Spiritfolk are living mortal people whose lineage has been shaped by spirits, old shrines, ancestor pacts, nature forces, local gods, or other persistent contact with the unseen world. Their exact manifestation is intentionally broad and may include luminous eyes, unusual markings, strange hair, omen-like sensations, or a subtle spiritual presence. They are not ghosts, undead, or automatically religious.

## In play

Your lineage carries a real Spirit affinity, but the source and meaning of that inheritance remain open to the character concept unless established elsewhere. This trait does not grant prophecy, a spirit companion, priestly authority, or a mandatory pact.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Seeing +8, Empathy +8, Spirit Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
