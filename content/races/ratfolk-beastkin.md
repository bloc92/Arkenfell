# Ratfolk Beastkin

Ratfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus rat ears or tail, quick hands, alert posture, and sharp senses. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Strong tolerance, scent awareness, and quiet movement help ratfolk function in difficult or crowded environments. Ratfolk ancestry does not make the character cowardly, criminal, unclean, or disease-ridden.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Smelling +8, Stealth +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
