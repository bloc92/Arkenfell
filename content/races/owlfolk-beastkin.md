# Owlfolk Beastkin

Owlfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus owl-like eyes, featherlike hair or markings, small ear tufts, and unusually still posture. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Night-adapted eyes, unusually still posture, and quiet movement favor observation and tracking in dim conditions. Scholarly interests are cultural or personal, not biologically required.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Hearing +11, Seeing +5, Stealth +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
