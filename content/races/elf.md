# Elf

Elves are long-lived mortal humanoids with pointed ears, graceful proportions, and naturally keen senses. Their ancestry does not determine culture, profession, morality, magical tradition, or preference for nature; those come from Origin Culture, Upbringing, and lived experience.

## In play

Your long-lived elven body carries sharp sight and hearing with naturally controlled movement. Do not infer archery training, animal affinity, Heroic Arcana, courtly behavior, or Lythael culture from Race alone.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Seeing +8, Hearing +8, Acrobatics +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
