# Sharkfolk Beastkin

Sharkfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus shark-like eyes, sharp human teeth, finlike markings, subtle rough skin texture, and water-ready posture. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

A water-ready build, rougher skin texture, strong scent sensitivity, and aquatic instincts favor powerful swimming and endurance in water. Do not grant underwater breathing or predatory aggression unless another mechanic establishes it.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Swimming +11, Smelling +5, Tolerance +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
