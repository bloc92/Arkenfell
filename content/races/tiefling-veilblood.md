# Tiefling — Veilblood

Veilblood tieflings descend from demonic lineages associated with desire, attention, temptation, dreams of possibility, and the pressures people reveal without meaning to. Their features may be graceful, uncanny, striking, or difficult to ignore, with unusual eyes and subtle infernal tells. This does not make them seductive, deceptive, immoral, or socially dominant by nature.

## In play

Your ancestry gives unusual sensitivity to attention, emotion, temptation, and social pressure. Enchantment Arcana appears as an inherited affinity for marks, bindings, wards, and persistent magical effects.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Empathy +8, Seeing +8, Enchantment Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
