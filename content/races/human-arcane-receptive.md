# Human — Arcane-Receptive

Humans possess unusually plastic physical, mental, social, and magical development, a quality often called Adaptable Potential. Rather than expressing one strongly specialized inherited form, human potential responds exceptionally well to environment, experience, training, hardship, and opportunity. This selection describes how that potential developed before play; it is not a human subspecies, ethnicity, culture, profession, or personality type. Your mind and body proved unusually receptive to magical development, allowing magical concepts, control exercises, and mana conditioning to take hold readily without determining which Arcana you possess.

## In play

You are Human. Your Adaptable Potential expressed as heightened magical receptivity. This does not grant an Arcana, spell, or ability; actual Arcana choices determine what magic you can use.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Arcane Lore +8, Self-Discipline +8, Mana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
