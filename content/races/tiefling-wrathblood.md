# Tiefling — Wrathblood

Wrathblood tieflings express a heavier, forceful demonic lineage associated with pressure, violent impact, endurance, and raw magical output. They may have thicker horns, denser musculature, powerful posture, or eyes that intensify when exerting themselves. Their ancestry does not force anger, violence, or a warrior life.

## In play

Your demonic ancestry favors forceful physical and magical expression. Power Arcana is an inherited affinity, while your choices determine whether that potential is ever used violently.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Might +8, Courage +8, Power Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
