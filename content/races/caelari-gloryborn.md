# Caelari — Gloryborn

Caelari (pronounced kay-LAR-ee) are mortal people whose ancestry carries an old celestial influence. They are not angels, chosen servants of a deity, automatically virtuous, or proof of divine favor. Gloryborn Caelari express a celestial inheritance associated with awe, presence, courage, and heroic magical resonance. They may appear unusually vivid or commanding even when dressed plainly.

## In play

Your celestial ancestry can lend supernatural weight to presence and Heroic affinity without making you noble, famous, righteous, charismatic in every situation, or destined for greatness.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Courage +8, Persuasion +8, Heroic Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
