# Boarkin Beastkin

Boarkin Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus boar ears, small tusks or tusk-like teeth, coarse hair, powerful shoulders, and a grounded stance. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Powerful shoulders, strong endurance, grounded posture, and keen scent favor hard physical effort and bodily leverage. Do not treat boarkin ancestry as automatic aggression, recklessness, or combat training.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Might +8, Smelling +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
