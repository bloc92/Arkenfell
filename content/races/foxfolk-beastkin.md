# Foxfolk Beastkin

Foxfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus fox ears, one or more foxlike tails, keen hearing, sharp eyes, and agile movement. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Sharp hearing, strong visual awareness, agile movement, and quiet footwork favor noticing danger and moving unobtrusively. Foxfolk ancestry does not imply deception, trickery, playfulness, or dishonesty.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Hearing +8, Seeing +8, Stealth +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
