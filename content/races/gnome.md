# Gnome

Gnomes are small mortal humanoids with fine hand control, sensitive touch, sharp close attention, and compact bodies suited to precise manipulation. Gnome ancestry does not automatically make someone an engineer, alchemist, scholar, inventor, or mage.

## In play

Your hands and tactile perception are naturally precise, making small details and delicate manipulation easier to manage. Technical knowledge still comes from actual training.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Searching +8, Touching +8, Legerdemain +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
