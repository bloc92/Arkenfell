# Dwarf

Dwarves are shorter and broader than humans, with dense builds, strong bones, powerful musculature, and long-lived mortal physiology. Dwarf ancestry does not automatically grant smithing, engineering, mining, weapon training, clan culture, or a mountain upbringing.

## In play

Your dense frame and strong bones favor raw effort, physical endurance, and close bodily leverage. Craft, military, and hold traditions must come from culture and lived experience rather than Race alone.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Might +8, Brawling +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
