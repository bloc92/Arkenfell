# Badgerfolk Beastkin

Badgerfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus badger ears, striped markings, strong hands, heavy shoulders, and a grounded stance. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Dense shoulders, strong hands, scent awareness, and a low center of gravity favor bracing, lifting, grappling, digging, and sustained exertion. Treat these as physical aptitudes, not a requirement that the character be stubborn or aggressive.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Might +8, Smelling +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
