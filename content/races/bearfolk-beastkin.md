# Bearfolk Beastkin

Bearfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus rounded bear ears, a dense build, broad shoulders, thick hair, and heavy movement. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

A dense build, heavy strength, durable frame, and strong scent sense favor lifting, carrying, resisting physical strain, and noticing nearby traces. Size may affect first impressions but does not dictate temperament.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Might +8, Tolerance +8, Smelling +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
