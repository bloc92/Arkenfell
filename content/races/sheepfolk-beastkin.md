# Sheepfolk Beastkin

Sheepfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus sheep ears, woolly hair, soft features, possible small horns, and a body naturally insulated by dense hair. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Dense woolly hair, sensitive hearing, scent awareness, and steady physical endurance help with exposure and environmental awareness. Gentleness, passivity, pastoral work, handcrafting, and obedience are not racial requirements.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Hearing +8, Smelling +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
