# Orc

Orcs are large, robust mortal humanoids typically marked by powerful builds, broad features, pronounced tusks or tusk-like teeth, and a physiology capable of sustained physical exertion. Skin tones vary widely by lineage and region. Orcs are a people rather than a warrior profession: culture, upbringing, status, work, faith, and temperament come from their actual lives.

## In play

Your size, tusks, and powerful frame can create an imposing first impression and support raw physical effort. Orc ancestry does not make you violent, militaristic, primitive, or culturally uniform.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Might +8, Tolerance +8, Intimidation +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
