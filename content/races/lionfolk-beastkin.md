# Lionfolk Beastkin

Lionfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus lion ears, a tail, mane-like hair, golden eyes, strong build, and confident posture. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

A strong build, mane-like hair, predatory eyes, and visibly powerful features can create formidable physical presence. Leadership, pride, courage, aggression, and social authority remain personal or cultural rather than racial.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Might +8, Intimidation +8, Seeing +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
