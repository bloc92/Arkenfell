# Hedgehogfolk Beastkin

Hedgehogfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus hedgehog ears, short spines through hair or shoulders, quick dark eyes, and compact resilience. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

A compact resilient build and short defensive spines make close handling awkward and favor bodily bracing and close leverage. The spines are physical features, not automatic armor or a damaging attack unless another mechanic says so.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Tolerance +8, Brawling +8, Might +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
