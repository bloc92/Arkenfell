# Elementborn — Ashborn

Elementborn are mortal people whose ancestry carries a persistent elemental expression strong enough to shape physiology and magical affinity. Elementborn are not elementals, and their element does not determine culture, morality, profession, or personality. Ashborn carry smoke, ash, soot, haze, and volcanic-air traits such as charcoal undertones, smoke-like markings, darkened sclera, muted scent, or fine ash appearing around them under magical stress.

## In play

Your body is unusually comfortable around smoke and airborne particulates and carries Smoke affinity. This does not grant immunity to suffocation, invisibility, or the ability to become smoke without explicit magic.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Stealth +8, Tolerance +8, Smoke Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
