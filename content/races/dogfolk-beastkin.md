# Dogfolk Beastkin

Dogfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus dog ears, a dog tail, keen hearing, scent awareness, and alert posture. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Keen scent and hearing make dogfolk especially good at noticing traces, changes, and nearby activity. Loyalty, obedience, friendliness, and emotional insight are personal traits rather than racial requirements.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Smelling +11, Hearing +5, Searching +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
