# Mousefolk Beastkin

Mousefolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus mouse ears, a thin tail, bright eyes, quick hands, and a small or slight humanlike build. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Sharp hearing, quick hands, and a slight humanlike build favor quiet movement and delicate manipulation. Nervousness, submissiveness, searching habits, or criminality are not inherent.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Stealth +8, Hearing +8, Legerdemain +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
