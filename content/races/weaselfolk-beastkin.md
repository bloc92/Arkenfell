# Weaselfolk Beastkin

Weaselfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus weasel ears, narrow features, a long tail, sharp eyes, and restless motion. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

A narrow build, rapid movement, flexible balance, and quick hands favor slipping through tight spaces and acting with speed and precision. Boldness or mischief is not biologically required.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Stealth +8, Acrobatics +8, Legerdemain +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
