# Caelari — Wardmarked

Caelari (pronounced kay-LAR-ee) are mortal people whose ancestry carries an old celestial influence. They are not angels, chosen servants of a deity, automatically virtuous, or proof of divine favor. Wardmarked Caelari express celestial ancestry associated with boundaries, guardianship, resistance, and protective force, sometimes visible through geometric birthmarks, pale-gold lines, or a calm pressure around active magic.

## In play

Your celestial inheritance carries a Protection affinity and a strong tendency toward controlled resistance. It does not impose an oath, duty, guardian role, or heroic personality.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Courage +8, Self-Discipline +8, Protection Arcana +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
