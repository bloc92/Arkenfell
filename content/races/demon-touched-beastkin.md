# Demon-Touched Beastkin

Demon-Touched Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus visible beastkin traits overlaid by horns, ember eyes, spade tails, heated clawlike nails, ash markings, or sharper instincts. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Infernal alterations such as horns, ember eyes, heated nails, ash markings, or a spade tail make demonic influence visibly present and support strong Fire affinity. This does not make the character evil, cruel, aggressive, or loyal to demons.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Fire Arcana +8, Intimidation +8, Tolerance +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
