# Human — Resourceful

Humans possess unusually plastic physical, mental, social, and magical development, a quality often called Adaptable Potential. Rather than expressing one strongly specialized inherited form, human potential responds exceptionally well to environment, experience, training, hardship, and opportunity. This selection describes how that potential developed before play; it is not a human subspecies, ethnicity, culture, profession, or personality type. Your development favored practical problem solving, noticing useful details, judging materials, and finding workable solutions with what was available.

## In play

You are Human. Your Adaptable Potential expressed through practical ingenuity rather than a specific trade; this does not grant professional mastery you never learned.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Searching +8, Handcrafting +8, Appraisal +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
