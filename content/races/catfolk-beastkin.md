# Catfolk Beastkin

Catfolk Beastkin are kemonomimi-style beastkin with fully humanlike faces and bodies plus cat ears, a cat tail, expressive pupils, quiet steps, and light balanced movement. They are not animal-headed, muzzled, furry-bodied, paw-handed, digitigrade animal-bodied, or full anthropomorphic animals.

## In play

Fine balance, quiet footwork, mobile ears, and a tail that assists body control favor stealth, agile movement, and hearing. Curiosity, aloofness, affection, and other personality traits remain entirely personal.

## Mechanical benefits

- **Modifiers:** Acrobatics +8, Stealth +8, Hearing +5

## Ancestry is not personality

This Race establishes inherited physical or magical qualities. It does not decide the character's culture, upbringing, profession, faith, morality, temperament, relationships, or choices unless a specific physical limitation is stated above.
