# Trait Catalog

Traits define ancestry, background, social position, training, aptitudes, life history, equipment choices, Arcana access, and progression. A trait can modify attributes, skills, resources, starting items, abilities, requirements, unlocks, exclusions, and narrative presentation.

This public catalog includes player-facing traits and trait families. **Hidden system markers, secret branch-state traits, and unrevealed campaign-lock traits are intentionally excluded from the public wiki even when they exist in Voyage.**

## Attribute Selection Traits

Character creation includes repeated attribute-allocation trait families:

- Attribute Foundation — Accuracy / Communication / Constitution / Dexterity / Fighting / Intelligence / Perception / Strength / Willpower
- Attribute Practice — the same nine attributes
- Attribute Training — the same nine attributes
- Attribute Focus — the same nine attributes
- Attribute Signature — the same nine attributes

Foundation, Practice, Training, and Focus entries generally grant **+1** to the named attribute. Signature entries grant **+2**.

## Arcana Traits

Player-facing Arcana traits grant access to the corresponding magical affinity. See the [Arcana Catalog](#arcana-catalog) for the standard, Rare, and Legendary Arcana names.

Arcana access is breadth, not mastery. Skill still determines control and practical competence.

## Combat Progression Families

The configured level-up pool contains multi-rank martial progressions such as:

- Archery Discipline → Expertise → Mastery → Apex
- Brawling Discipline → Expertise → Mastery → Apex
- Dueling Discipline → Expertise → Mastery → Apex
- Light Blade Discipline → Expertise → Mastery → Apex
- Staff Fighting Discipline → Expertise → Mastery → Apex
- Sling Discipline → Expertise → Mastery → Apex
- Shield Discipline → Expertise → Mastery → Apex

Additional specialist and advanced progression traits include Arcane Scholarship, Arcana mastery paths, mounted combat specialties, engineering and rune specialties, investigative magic, relic use, warding, battle healing, beast-binding, navigation, social expertise, and resource-development traits.

## Resource Development Traits

### Deep Mana Well
Repeated casting, recovery, and disciplined magical exertion have expanded the amount of Mana you can hold safely. Grants **+20 Mana** and requires level 15 plus Self-Discipline 30. It increases reserve rather than Arcana breadth or automatic spell control.

### Vital Reserve
Hardship, recovery, and physical conditioning have increased physical reserve. Grants **+20 Health** and requires level 15 plus Tolerance 30. It does not grant immunity to wounds, disease, poison, blood loss, or medical consequence.

### Tireless Reserve
Long travel and repeated exertion expand sustained physical reserve. The character can carry more Stamina through work and danger but still pays normal Stamina costs and requires real recovery.

## Exploration and Survival Traits

### Survivor
Practical experience with wilderness, weather, hunger, thirst, exposure, dangerous terrain, and long travel. Grants **Natural Lore +20**.

### Swimmer
Comfort and training in rivers, lakes, oceans, flooded tunnels, marsh water, surf, and underwater passages. Grants **Swimming +17** and **Tolerance +5**.

### Tracker
Training in following footprints, broken vegetation, scents, spoor, travel patterns, magical residue, and signs of movement. Grants **Tracking +16** and **Natural Lore +5**.

### Sure Leaper
Experienced at judging and clearing gaps, broken bridges, ravines, rooftops, fences, and unstable terrain. Grants **Jumping +15** and **Acrobatics +5**.

### Trap Handler
Training to notice, disarm, bypass, reset, or safely trigger traps, alarms, snares, pressure plates, tripwires, needles, and hidden mechanisms.

## Background and Life-Path Examples

### Academy Candidate
You were considered for academy entry, magical training, formal schooling, or institutional mentorship during your preteen years. Grants **Arcane Lore +6**.

### Dungeoneering Hireling
You took early work around ruins, vaults, caves, old temples, monster lairs, sealed sites, or dangerous underground spaces. Grants **Searching +5** and **Traps +3**.

### Left Home Recently
Your departure from home is still recent enough to shape how you compare new places with what you left behind. Grants **Stamina +8** and **Cultural Lore +6**.

### Mercenary Recruit
You joined a mercenary band, sellsword company, caravan guard group, private retinue, or paid fighting force. Grants **Health +5**, **Stamina +11**, and **Bargaining +3**.

### Merchant Clerk
You worked as a clerk, junior trader, shop hand, caravan bookkeeper, warehouse aide, or merchant-house employee. Grants **Bargaining +8** and **Archival Research +6**.

### Merchant House Standing
Your standing comes from trade, contracts, ownership, ledgers, patronage, or a merchant house. Grants **Bargaining +8** and **Archival Research +8**.

### First Quest Completed
You completed your first real quest, contract, expedition, rescue, delivery, monster job, relic errand, or dangerous adult task. Grants **Military Lore +5** and **Leadership +3**.

### First Quest Failed
Your first real quest or dangerous adult undertaking failed or ended badly. Grants **Tolerance +5** and **Military Lore +3**.

### Friend Lost to War
War took a friend, peer, sibling-like companion, rival, or someone important to your youth. Grants **Empathy +3** and **Military Lore +3**.

### Frontier Freeholder
Your social standing comes from a homestead, border farm, frontier claim, new settlement, ranger route, or family holding near danger. Grants **Natural Lore +5** and **Military Lore +5**.

### Heirloom Blade Inherited
You inherited, were shown, or were promised a family blade or meaningful old armament. Grants **Dueling +3** and **Heraldry +3**.

## Equipment Traits

The trait catalog also contains player-facing starting-equipment choices such as bows, crossbows, lances, slings, staves, axes, spears, shields, handwraps, and other martial gear. These traits grant the corresponding starting item rather than representing a secret character state.

Because the live Voyage trait library is very large, this page is organized by player-facing families rather than placing every internal record in the sidebar. Exact trait definitions remain authoritative in Voyage; future wiki expansion can split specific families into dedicated pages without changing their canonical names.
