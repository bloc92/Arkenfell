# Arkenfell

Arkenfell is the setting reference for this Voyage world. This wiki is intended to help players understand the people, places, institutions, cultures, history, and terminology their characters may reasonably encounter.

## What belongs here

This wiki contains **player-facing canon** by default: established information that can be safely read without revealing hidden motives, unresolved mysteries, secret histories, or campaign-specific outcomes. Additional GM-only sections become visible only while the site is in GM Mode.

The wiki is a reference source, not a replacement for Voyage's own World Lore and structured world data. Voyage still needs the relevant setting knowledge encoded inside the game so the narrator can retrieve and use it during play.

## Knowledge levels

Articles may identify how widely information is known inside the setting. A fact being available to players in this wiki does not mean every NPC knows it.

- **Common** — broadly known by ordinary people in the relevant culture or region.
- **Regional** — broadly known within a particular realm, region, or community.
- **Educated** — likely known to educated people, officials, scholars, clergy, or specialists.
- **Specialist** — requires a profession, discipline, institution, or focused study.
- **Restricted** — known to a limited group but not treated as a GM secret.

## Canon and campaigns

The wiki records setting canon. Individual Voyage campaigns can diverge from that baseline through player choices, quest outcomes, deaths, succession changes, political decisions, and other state changes. Those campaign outcomes belong in the campaign's game state rather than being silently rewritten into general setting canon.

:::gm

## GM Reference Notes

### Narrative Notes

- Keep campaign outcomes separate from baseline setting canon unless a campaign is later promoted into official canon.
- Do not let privileged institutions, factions, or NPCs gain authority merely because their beliefs or preferences are narratively forceful; their actual powers must remain consistent with established canon.
- When a public article intentionally describes an in-world belief rather than objective truth, state the distinction explicitly in its GM-only section.

### Voyage Implementation

- The wiki remains a human-readable source of canon and does not replace Voyage World Lore.
- Facts required for reliable narration should also exist in the appropriate Voyage structures, including World Lore, Factions, NPCs, Realms, Regions, Locations, Narrative Events, triggers, Game Modes, or AI Instructions as appropriate.
- Campaign-specific state changes should remain in game state, triggers, quests, or other deterministic runtime state rather than silently rewriting general setting canon.
