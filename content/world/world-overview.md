# World Overview

Arkenfell is a high-fantasy world of royal courts, rural communities, magical institutions, dangerous roads, ancient ruins, trade powers, temple law, monster societies, hidden factions, and ordinary lives shaped by extraordinary forces.

The currently mapped center of play is **Eldaven**, a continent divided among kingdoms, empires, republics, religious states, magical powers, clan territories, and contested borderlands. Its societies differ in law, custom, faith, trade, warfare, and attitudes toward magic.

## A World of Places and Consequences

Arkenfell is designed as a living open world rather than a single predetermined quest line. Characters begin with a place in society, personal history, relationships, opportunities, and pressures. Their choices determine which causes, conflicts, and discoveries become important.

Political decisions can change reputations and alliances. Travel can uncover local problems, lost sites, rival claims, and unfamiliar customs. Magic can influence education, status, religion, law, research, healing, war, and daily work.

## Eldaven

Eldaven contains many distinct regions and political powers. Valenreach is a common starting point, but it is only one part of the continent.

See [Eldaven](#eldaven) for the continental overview and the **Realms** and **Places** sections for individual powers, regions, settlements, and landmarks.

## Using the Wiki

This wiki presents established setting canon in a form intended for human reference.

- **World** explains the setting at its broadest level.
- **Realms** covers political powers and their societies.
- **Places** covers regions, settlements, landmarks, and routes.
- **People** and **Factions** cover the individuals and organizations shaping events.
- **Magic** and **Faith** explain supernatural traditions and institutions.
- **Rules & Play** explains the character systems used during play.

Player mode avoids deliberate spoilers. GM Mode adds hidden motives, unrevealed history, campaign notes, and other gated material where available.
