# Story Starts

Browse all **40 configured Story Starts** in Arkenfell. Each menu begins with a short, spoiler-free premise. Open a Story Start to see its starting location, what kind of play may develop from it, and introductions to important characters where the start has an established cast.

## Fully Authored and World Exploration Starts

**Fully Authored Story Starts** have dedicated casts and long-form storylines built specifically around their opening premise. They remain responsive to the player's decisions, but their central situations, major developments, locations, and supporting characters receive extensive authored support.

**World Exploration Story Starts** provide focused openings in particular regions, cultures, professions, or conflicts. They are designed to grow outward through the wider world, local relationships, travel, and the player's own priorities rather than following one dedicated authored storyline.

Character introductions describe only what a player may reasonably know before or near the beginning of the start. Select a character's name to open their full entry in the People directory. Hidden premises, quest scripts, private motives, and internal narrator instructions are not included.
