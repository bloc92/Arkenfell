# Story Starts

Browse all **40 configured Story Starts** in Arkenfell. Each entry gives the player-facing premise, the configured starting location, and a short, spoiler-free look at the kinds of choices and developments that may await further into play.

## Fully Authored and World Exploration Starts

**Fully Authored Story Starts** have dedicated casts and long-form storylines built specifically around their opening premise. They can still develop differently through the player's decisions, but their central situations, major developments, and supporting characters receive extensive authored support.

**World Exploration Story Starts** provide focused openings in particular regions, cultures, professions, or conflicts. They are designed to grow outward through the wider world, local relationships, travel, and the player's own priorities rather than following one dedicated authored storyline.

Where a Story Start has an established cast, it also lists the NPCs a player can expect to encounter. Select a name to open that character directly in the People directory. Hidden premises, quest scripts, NPC motives, and internal narrator instructions are not included.
