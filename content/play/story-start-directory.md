# Story Starts

Browse all **40 configured Story Starts** in Arkenfell. Each entry gives the player-facing premise, the configured starting location, and a short, spoiler-free look at the kinds of choices and developments that may await further into play.

Where a Story Start has an established cast, it also lists the NPCs a player can expect to encounter. Select a name to open that character directly in the People directory. Hidden premises, quest scripts, NPC motives, and internal narrator instructions are not included.
