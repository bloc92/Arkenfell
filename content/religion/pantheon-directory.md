# Pantheon Directory

Eldaven recognizes many divine powers rather than one universal church. Moral/Theological Standing describes how cultures and institutions generally treat a deity's worship; it is not a fixed cosmic alignment or proof that every individual agrees.

## National and Cultural Benefactors

### Ardoval, the Oath-Crowned
**Standing:** Revered Benevolent  
**Associated with:** Valenreach; oaths, crowns, rightful rule, inheritance, courts, sworn duty, guardianship, noble burden.  
**Symbol:** A crowned sword across an open oath-ring.

Eldmark Kingdom currently has **no single named divine benefactor established as canon**. Its public devotion centers on storm shrines, ancestor oaths, royal law-stones, and war-dead barrows.

## Widely Honored Powers

### Hestrelle, the Gentle Hearth
**Standing:** Revered Benevolent  
Goddess of hearth, mercy, healing, childbirth, refuge, food, wells, family, rural prosperity, and ordinary kindness.  
**Symbol:** A clay lamp cupped between two hands.

### Morcant, the Last Witness
**Standing:** Accepted Necessary  
God of death, funerals, ancestors, graves, last rites, ghosts, oathbound dead, buried memory, final testimony, and proper endings.  
**Symbol:** A closed eye above a black candle.

### Tharvane, the Wild Horn
**Standing:** Accepted Necessary  
God of beasts, monsters, forests, hunting, livestock, seasons, predator and prey, wild fertility, and territory beyond court law.  
**Symbol:** A stag skull crowned with green leaves.

### Elaris, the Silver Veil
**Standing:** Accepted Ambivalent  
Goddess of moon, dreams, prophecy, omens, hidden roads, fate-glimpses, nightmares, sleepwalking, and difficult warnings.  
**Symbol:** A silver veil drawn across a crescent moon.

### Nysera, the Turning Coin
**Standing:** Accepted Ambivalent  
Goddess of luck, chance, thieves, gamblers, sudden reversals, wagers, escapes, lost causes, and survival by wit.  
**Symbol:** A two-faced coin spinning above a dagger point.

### Caelwyn, the First Song
**Standing:** Revered Benevolent  
Deity of art, music, love, inspiration, beauty, performance, festivals, courtly romance, public memory, and stories that outlive witnesses.  
**Symbol:** A golden harp-string crossing an open mouth.

### Vaunthis, the Nameless Door
**Standing:** Feared but Recognized  
God of exile, forbidden thresholds, lost paths, erased names, vanished cities, dangerous crossings, and doors that should not open.  
**Symbol:** A blank door without handle or hinge.

## Forbidden Malignant Powers

### Nharvek, the Chain King
God of slavery, domination, tyranny, forced obedience, soul-binding, fear, and broken autonomy.  
**Symbol:** A black iron chain wrapped around a crown.

### Velkora, the Hollow Queen
Goddess of forced undeath, lichdom, corpse slavery, hollow immortality, blood hunger without restraint, forbidden resurrection, and refusing death at the expense of others.  
**Symbol:** A pale crown above an open grave.

### Malgrath, the Rot Beneath
God of plague, corruption, famine, poisoned land, spiritual decay, blight, rot-cults, and demon-wound geography.  
**Symbol:** A cracked black root dripping green sickness.

### Ossyra, the Knife Behind the Smile
Goddess of betrayal, murder, false friendship, assassination, poison, secret cults, and treachery wearing a familiar face.  
**Symbol:** A smiling mask pierced from behind by a narrow blade.

## Undeath and the Gods

Undeath is not automatically evil in Arkenfell. Ghosts, ancestor dead, and oathbound dead may fall within Morcant's domain when they preserve memory, duty, testimony, or unfinished grief. Velkora represents forced undeath, corpse slavery, hollow immortality, and undeath imposed at another's expense.

Vampires are morally varied. Cruel vampires who treat mortals as livestock drift toward Velkora, while restrained vampires and blood-law traditions are more closely associated with Noctheris. The theological concern is coercion, uncontrolled predation, soul violation, corpse desecration, and spreading undeath by force.
