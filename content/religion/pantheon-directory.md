# Faith and Pantheon

Browse all **26 dedicated deity records** in Arkenfell. Search by divine name, associated country or culture, theological standing, symbol, worshippers, or domain.

The directory groups national and cultural benefactors, widely honored powers, and forbidden malignant powers. A deity's standing describes how cultures and institutions generally treat that power; it is not an objective cosmic alignment that every person must accept.
