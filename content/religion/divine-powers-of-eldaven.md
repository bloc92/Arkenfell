# The Divine Powers of Eldaven

The peoples of Eldaven recognize many divine powers rather than one universal church. Nations and enduring cultures often claim a benefactor god connected to founding law, survival, rulership, identity, or sacred tradition, while ordinary people may pray to several gods over the course of their lives.

Arkenfell describes gods through **Moral/Theological Standing** rather than a fixed cosmic alignment. Common standings include Revered Benevolent, Revered Ambivalent, Accepted Necessary, Accepted Ambivalent, Controversial Patron, Feared but Recognized, and Forbidden Malignant. These categories describe how cultures and institutions treat worship; they are not an objective rule that every individual agrees with.

Among the major Eldaven powers, Valenreach honors Ardoval, the Oath-Crowned; the Tide Empire honors Maelthar, the Storm-Bearer; the Dreadmark Dominion honors Dravok, the Iron Will; the Lythael Elven Dominion honors Lythirien, the Root of Memory; the Solmire Concord honors Serrava, the Open Hand; the Solarian Theocracy honors Solcaris, the Unblinking Sun; and the Arcanum Ascendancy honors Myrathis, the Veiled Flame.
