# Arcana Tiers and Access

Arcana are grouped by rarity and accessibility. These tiers describe how unusual an affinity is, how readily it can be chosen or taught, and how the wider world may react to it. They do **not** measure a caster's skill.

A practiced mage with one Standard Arcana can be far more capable than an inexperienced person who has somehow awakened a Rare or Legendary affinity.

## Standard Arcana

Standard Arcana form the established foundation of magical practice in Arkenfell.

- A new character may optionally begin with up to four Standard Arcana.
- Academies, temples, military schools, private tutors, and experienced practitioners may teach them.
- Being Standard does not make an Arcana weak. It means the affinity is recognized, teachable, and available through ordinary character creation.
- Each Arcana still requires training before it can be used reliably under pressure.

## Rare Arcana

Rare Arcana are not normal character-creation choices. They usually enter a character's story through a meaningful discovery, awakening, teacher, relic, crisis, ritual, transformation, or other established event.

Their rarity can bring attention from scholars, temples, rulers, factions, collectors, or enemies. That attention depends on the Arcana and the society encountering it.

Gaining access to a Rare Arcana does not grant instant mastery. Its skill must still be developed through use and training.

## Legendary Arcana

Legendary Arcana are exceptionally uncommon and consequential. They are not normal beginner selections and should emerge only from major events with a credible place in the story.

A Legendary affinity may alter how powerful institutions understand a character, but it does not make that character invincible, morally important, royal, prophetic, or destined to succeed. Legendary magic still obeys its own domain, depends on the relevant skill, and carries risks and consequences.

## Forbidden and Restricted Magic

**Forbidden** and **restricted** are not additional rarity tiers.

They describe how a realm, faith, academy, order, or other authority treats a form of magic. An Arcana may be outlawed in one country, licensed in another, and openly studied somewhere else. Particular rituals or applications can also be forbidden even when the underlying Arcana is legal.

## Story-Unlocked Arcana

**Story-unlocked** describes how access is gained, not how powerful the Arcana is.

A Standard, Rare, or Legendary Arcana can be unlocked during play when events genuinely establish that change. A character might train under a teacher, survive an awakening, complete a ritual, bond with a relic, undergo a transformation, or earn access through a quest.

The story must establish the cause. Simply declaring that a character has awakened a new affinity is not enough.

## Access, Skill, and Abilities

These are three separate parts of magical development:

1. **Arcana access** determines which magical affinity the character possesses.
2. **Arcana skill** measures training, control, reliability, and practical competence in that affinity.
3. **Abilities** are specific techniques that become available when their requirements are met.

Knowing several Arcana gives breadth. Raising an Arcana skill gives mastery. Unlocking abilities gives the character defined ways to apply that mastery.
