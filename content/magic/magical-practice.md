# Magical Practice and Education

Magic is useful, dangerous, politically valuable, and physically demanding. Formal institutions exist because talent without control can hurt the caster, bystanders, institutions, or the wider community.

## Magical Education

Academies teach theory, control, safety procedure, ethics, history, recordkeeping, and supervised practice. Temples teach sacred discipline, ritual form, witness law, mercy, restraint, and doctrine. Military schools teach formation casting, dueling control, defensive wards, battlefield signals, and the discipline required not to kill allies by mistake. Merchant states may emphasize contract magic, seals, audit methods, and practical risk.

Institutions disagree about what responsible magic means:

- **Crownspire** emphasizes discipline, records, court service, and lawful usefulness.
- **Arcanum Ascendancy** emphasizes classification, research, controlled experimentation, and identifying valuable talent.
- **Solarian Theocracy** emphasizes doctrine, witness, purity, mercy, and sanctioned use.
- **Dreadmark Dominion** emphasizes obedience and weaponized utility.
- **Lythael** emphasizes restraint, memory, etiquette, and long-term consequence.
- **Solmire** emphasizes reliable contracts, recoverable losses, and accountable promises.

## Fatigue and Backlash

Spellcasting requires attention, breath, body control, emotional regulation, training, and resource management. Minor casting may leave warmth, tingling, focus strain, thirst, trembling, headache, numbness, breathlessness, or emotional spillover. Stronger casting can produce exhaustion, bruised nerves, burns, frostbite, vertigo, bleeding, temporary sensory distortion, loss of fine control, or collapse.

**Overchanneling** occurs when a caster pushes beyond safe control. Causes can include too much power, too little training, poor footing, panic, lack of sleep, injury, hostile interference, unstable runes, or a dangerous environment.

Backlash is proportional. A novice mistake should not automatically destroy a city, and a master's catastrophic failure should not become harmless merely because the caster is powerful.
