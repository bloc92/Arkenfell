# Magic Overview

Magic in Arkenfell is powerful, varied, teachable, and woven into society. It can shape combat, travel, healing, scholarship, religion, politics, trade, reputation, and daily work.

Magic is not a single measure of power. A character's magical development has several separate parts:

- **Arcana access** determines which magical affinities the character possesses.
- **Arcana skill** represents training, control, reliability, and depth.
- **Abilities** are particular techniques with their own requirements and effects.
- **Arcane Lore** represents knowledge about magic rather than an affinity of its own.

## Arcana

Arcana are named magical affinities such as Fire, Healing, Rune, Divination, or Protection. A character may know several Arcana without being equally skilled in all of them.

## Training and Control

Talent without control can endanger the caster and everyone nearby. Academies, temples, military schools, private tutors, and other traditions teach theory, safety, ethics, discipline, and practical use.

A character with one deeply trained Arcana may be much more capable than someone with several undeveloped affinities. Rarity creates access and social consequences; it does not replace practice.

## Magic in Society

Different realms and institutions treat magic differently. The same practice may be celebrated, licensed, monitored, restricted, or forbidden depending on local law, doctrine, and political interest.

Powerful or unusual magic can attract teachers, researchers, rulers, temples, factions, rivals, and opportunists. An Arcana never determines a character's morality, destiny, profession, or social allegiance by itself.
