# Power Arcana

Power Arcana is a Willpower-based magical discipline centered on amplifying force beyond natural limits through raw magical pressure, enhanced physical output, kinetic reinforcement, and impact escalation.

## What it does

Power Arcana strengthens an action. It can reinforce a strike, leap, defense, spell impact, or other immediate exertion so that the action carries greater force than it otherwise could.

It does **not** replace the underlying skill or control required to perform that action.

## What it is not

Power Arcana is not the same thing as ordinary physical strength. **Might** represents raw physical strength, while relevant **Fighting skills** represent weapon technique and trained combat execution.

Power Arcana is also a distinct Arcana and should not be confused with **Force Arcana** merely because both can involve force in ordinary language.
