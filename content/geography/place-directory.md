# Places of Arkenfell

Browse the public Arkenfell world's **25 regions and 353 locations**. Search by name, region, location type, or descriptive keyword, or begin with a regional overview and open its location roster directly.

![Map of Eldaven](Images/Arkenfell_map/Eldaven.png)

Every displayed record is explicitly sourced from **Arkenfell**. Regions, locations, or areas found only in **Arkenfell Private** are deliberately excluded from player mode.

Each of the **353 locations now opens as its own article**. Location articles keep their region visible and place configured areas beneath the parent location rather than creating a separate article for every room, district, or site.

Entries that Voyage currently marks as unknown remain available behind a spoiler warning. This keeps established places findable without forcing their descriptions on readers.

## Areas

Areas belong beneath their parent location rather than beside regions and locations. The current public wiki export does not yet contain the structured area records from Voyage; location articles are prepared to display them as soon as that source data is available, without changing the article structure or creating standalone area pages.
