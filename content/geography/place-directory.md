# Places of Arkenfell

This directory gathers named places that have enough player-facing information to be useful. It will expand as regions and locations receive proper public descriptions; a name appearing in private World data alone is not enough to invent details for it here.

## Valenreach

### [Highcourt Armathen](#highcourt-armathen)
The royal palace of Valenreach, seat of the Crown, and home of the royal household. A full article is available.

### Crownlake Heartlands
A central Valenreach region where Crown politics, noble interests, institutions, trade, and faction activity overlap.

## Wild and Ancient Regions

### Deepgreen Wilds
A woodland region connected with the Rootbound Moot, the Verdant Circle, dryads, old groves, and enduring nature ties.

### Greyhaven
A place retained in public warning myths involving darkness, memory, hidden flame, soul-silence, and forgotten pact lines. Those stories do not by themselves establish their literal truth.

## Strongholds and Seats

### Mourning Bell Bastion
The base associated with the Mourning Bell Wardens, their patrols, warning bells, and protective military service.

### Kar Anvilroot
A seat of the Ironroot Holds associated with the Anvilroot ruling family, clan authority, oathcraft, and dwarven political life.

## Coasts and Islands

### Stormpearl Haven
A Stormpearl center associated with free captains, storm-road leadership, ships, reefs, navigators, and lighthouse families.
